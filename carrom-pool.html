How to Play

🎯 Objective: Pot all your assigned carrom pucks (White or Black) before your opponent!


🎛️ Controls:

  • Use the Bottom Slider to position your striker along baseline.
  • Touch & Drag back from striker to aim & set shot power.
  • Release touch to shoot!

⚠️ Foul Penalty: Potting the striker returns a puck to the center circle.
